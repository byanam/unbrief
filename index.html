[ PROTOCOL // 002 ] — 3-STAGE WORKFLOW

From raw client chaos to defensible contract.

Eliminate lost weekends spent formatting Keynote decks or recalculating day rates in spreadsheets. Deliver enterprise-grade reverse-briefs while your competitor is still drafting an intake email.

01 / INTAKE ENGINE

Paste brief or discovery audio

Drop in whatever the client sent: a 45-minute Fireflies or Otter audio transcript, a rambling bulleted email, or Slack call notes. Unbrief filters out buzzwords and pinpoints commercial requirements.

02 / AMBIGUITY & PRICING SCAN

AI qualifies, scopes, and prices

The engine detects unspoken dependencies, constructs 3 structured tiers (Good / Better / Best), pairs deliverables with your blended rate cards, and writes contractual out-of-scope boundaries.

03 / COMMERCIAL DISPATCH

Clean proposal out & e-sign

Deliver an interactive proposal hosted on your agency's custom domain or export to executive PDF. The client selects their desired tier, reviews milestones, and executes a legally binding e-signature.
