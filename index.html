How It Works

From raw brief to signed contract in 3 steps

Stop spending your weekends cobbling together Keynote decks. Deliver enterprise-grade reverse-briefs before your competitor opens a blank doc.

STEP 01

Paste brief or discovery notes

Drop in whatever the client sent: a 45-minute Fireflies or Otter audio transcript, a chaotic bulleted email, or Slack call notes. Unbrief filters buzzwords and identifies real commercial goals.

STEP 02

AI qualifies, scopes, and prices

The engine detects hidden dependencies, constructs 3 structured pricing tiers (Good / Better / Best), matches your agency rate cards, and writes contractual out-of-scope boundaries.

STEP 03

Clean proposal out & e-sign

Deliver an interactive proposal hosted on your custom domain or export to PDF. The client selects their desired tier, reviews milestones, and executes a legally binding e-signature.
